🔧 What I do

Real help for real people

I come to your home. We sit down together and sort it out. Here’s the sort of thing I do most weeks:

📱

Set up a phone or tablet

New device feeling overwhelming? I’ll transfer your contacts, photos, and apps — and show you the basics so you feel in control.

✉

Email & internet basics

Set up your email on any device, explain what’s a scam and what’s not, and help you browse safely and confidently.

💻

Fix a slow computer

If your laptop or desktop has slowed to a crawl, I’ll find out why and get it running properly again — no “reinstall Windows” shortcuts.

🖨

Printer problems

Wireless printer won’t connect? Paper jam you can’t figure out? I’ll get it working — and show you what to do next time.

🔄

Explain updates & pop-ups

“Update available” — should I click it? What’s it changing? I’ll translate the technical messages into plain English.

🛠

Something else?

If it plugs in, connects to Wi-Fi, or runs on a battery — give me a call. If I can’t help, I’ll tell you honestly and point you to someone who can.

🚫 What I don’t do

Managing expectations

I’m here for you — not for servers, code, or full-scale office IT. Here’s what’s outside my lane:

✕

Web hosting & servers

I don’t run or fix servers, manage hosting accounts, or build websites.

✕

Office / business IT

If you need a full office network, business email server, or multi-user setup, I’ll point you to a specialist.

✕

Programming & code

I don’t write software, build apps, or fix broken code.

✕

Repairs & soldering

Hardware repairs, screen replacements, and solder work are best left to a dedicated repair shop.

If what you need is in that list, I’ll tell you — no wasting your time. I can probably recommend someone local who does it.
